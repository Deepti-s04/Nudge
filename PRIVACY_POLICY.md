# Nudge Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

Nudge is a Chrome extension designed to help users manage distractions, block selected websites, and track focus sessions.

## Information Used

Nudge uses information needed to provide its features, including:
- Websites selected by users for blocking or restriction.
- Website domains visited during active focus sessions.
- Time spent on selected websites and focus session history.
- Extension settings and session state.

## How Information Is Used

This information is used to enforce website restrictions, calculate website usage durations, maintain focus sessions, and display usage statistics in the dashboard.

## Data Storage and Sharing

Nudge uses Chrome extension storage to maintain its settings and tracking data. The developer does not sell users' personal information.
